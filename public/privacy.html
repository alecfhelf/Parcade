Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

This policy explains what Parcade collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we collect what the app needs to run your rounds, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address, your first name, and when you agreed to these policies.
  • Game info: round and season names, player names, stick-figure colors, scores, handicaps or usual scores, challenge results, chat messages, bet descriptions and amounts, and payouts.
  • Guest sessions: a random ID so you can play without an account.
  • Settings on your device: things like sound, volume, and scorekeeper preferences are stored in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • Technical info: our hosting and database providers automatically log basics like IP addresses and browser type to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

Only to run Parcade: saving and showing rounds, seasons, chat, and payouts, keeping you signed in, and fixing problems. We don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • People in your round can see the names, scores, chat, bets, and results in that round. Members of a season can see that season's standings and money stats.
  • We use service providers to run the app: Supabase (database and accounts), Netlify (hosting), and Google Fonts and jsDelivr (fonts and code libraries). They process data only to provide those services.
  • We may share information if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is active. To delete your account and personal information, email support@parcade.golf. Rounds you played with others may be kept with your name removed so their records still work. Guest data on your device can be cleared by clearing your browser's site data.

Children

Parcade isn't meant for anyone under 13, and money features are only for people 21 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect your information, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email support@parcade.golf.
